Permission boundary

Mentor privileges remain explicit

  • Mentor-owned drafts may be published directly, with an explicit privileged-publication audit event.
  • Mentors may still submit their work for independent review when a second pair of eyes is useful.
  • A mentor cannot approve their own submitted review copy.
  • A mentor sees student or tutor workspaces only when those roles are separately assigned.
